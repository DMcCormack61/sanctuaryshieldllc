Our Services

Comprehensive safety and security solutions designed specifically for churches and Christian organizations.

View All Services Schedule Free Consultation

Every service is customized to your church’s size, location, culture, and specific needs. We work alongside your leadership to protect your testimony, people, and resources while keeping the focus on ministry.

Service Description
Security & Safety Assessment On-site physical, cyber, and testimony vulnerability assessment, facility walkthrough, safety and security risk review, and written report with recommended Courses of Action.
Risk Assessment and Risk Management Plan Detailed, prioritized risk registry with recommended mitigation for each risk.
Incident Preparedness with Policies & Procedures Active shooter / violent / disruptive incident preparedness, response protocols, tabletop and crawl-walk-run exercises.
Establishing a Volunteer Security & Safety Team Recruit, screen, train, and organize a volunteer team (includes tailored policies, drills, and communication plan).
Establishing a Safety & Security Ministry Provide structure, policy, procedures, and overall ministry framework tailored to your church. Develop ministry mission statement and integration plan with existing ministries.
Grant Writing Complete application package for the DHS Nonprofit Security Grant Program (includes risk assessment, budget justification, and submission support).
Ongoing Support Phone/Zoom support, quarterly on-site check-ins, remote monthly meetings, plan updates, emergency consultation, and annual policy refresh. Cost varies by support level.
Consulting Expert consulting on any safety and security topic (building alterations, new construction plans, camera placement, cybersecurity tools & procedures, etc.).
Armed Security Guard (PA Act 235) Trained, armed security guard for temporary or ongoing coverage. Open or concealed carry, uniformed or incognito options available.
Unarmed Security Guard Service Trained unarmed security guard for temporary or ongoing coverage. Uniformed or incognito options available.
Security Camera Planning Expert guidance on camera placement, quantity, hardware recommendations, and third-party monitoring options.
Security Camera Installation Full professional installation, system validation, and monitoring plan for your facility.
Security Camera Monitoring Flexible monitoring plans ranging from selected hours to 24/7/365 coverage.
