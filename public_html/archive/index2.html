Christian Schools & Academies

Christian schools combine the open, welcoming nature of a church with the daily responsibilities of educating and protecting children. This creates unique vulnerabilities that require specialized, biblically grounded solutions.

Risks to School Testimony

Parents choose Christian education for spiritual formation and safety. Incidents can devastate enrollment and trust.

  • • Enrollment decline and donor withdrawal
  • • Accreditation and regulatory challenges
  • • Long-term reputational harm

Risks to Students & Staff

Daily student traffic, after-school programs, and faculty responsibilities increase exposure.

  • • Higher volume of visitors and traffic
  • • Mandatory reporting for educators
  • • Comprehensive background screening

Risks to School Resources

Tight budgets make financial impacts particularly painful.

  • • Lawsuits and insurance increases
  • • Loss of funding or accreditation
  • • Cyber risks to student data
