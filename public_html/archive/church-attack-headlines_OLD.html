JUL 9, 2026

Video shows NYC houses of worship targeted in Molotov cocktail attacks

Police in Queens arrested a 36-year-old man accused of firebombing two houses of worship overnight. Surveillance video shows the suspect throwing a Molotov cocktail at Iglesia Bautista El Mesias in Ozone Park; he then targeted a Kingdom Hall of Jehovah's Witnesses in nearby Woodhaven. Additional incendiary devices were recovered from his backpack. No injuries reported; fires were quickly extinguished.

Michigan Church Shooting and Fire: What We Know

At least four people were killed and eight injured when a gunman rammed a vehicle into a Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints meetinghouse in Grand Blanc Township, opened fire during worship, and set the building ablaze.
